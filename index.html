    📖 Travel Logistics: Vacation Budget Allocation

    Efficient itinerary planning balances geographical transit clusters with financial buffer allocations to minimize vacation stress:

    • The 40/30/20/10 Budget Framework: Experienced travel planners generally allocate 40% of the total budget to transport and accommodation, 30% to food and dining experiences, 20% to tours and museum entries, and 10% as an emergency buffer.
    • Geographical Clustering: Organize each day's itinerary around a tight neighborhood hub. Attempting to cross a metropolitan center multiple times a day squanders 30% of daylight hours in transit.
    • Buffer Windows: Schedule at least one 90-minute unscheduled window daily to absorb flight delays, train connections, and spontaneous neighborhood discoveries.

    Travel references: Steves R. (2020) Europe Through the Back Door; World Tourism Organization (UNWTO) Travel Economics Guidelines.

    ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

    Can I access my travel itinerary offline without internet?

    Yes. The itinerary planner operates with 100% local browser caching, allowing you to view and adjust your travel schedules on airplanes or remote locations without cellular data.

    How does the per-person expense splitting work?

    By specifying the number of travelers in your party, the planner automatically divides shared accommodation, transit, and overall group expenses into individual per-person shares.

    Can I print or save my travel plan as a PDF document?

    Yes. Tapping the Print/PDF button optimizes the schedule into a clean printable itinerary format, eliminating navigation bars and ads for easy travel binder packing.

    Are my vacation locations or flight details stored online?

    No. All destination names, dates, flight times, and financial budgets are saved solely in your local browser storage. No itinerary data is transferred to external servers.
